Everyday tasks. Your own rhythm.

Why choose Time Since?

Choose Time Since for chores, filter changes, haircuts and other everyday tasks you want to remember. Keep the full reset history, see your average time between resets, and let learned Due/Overdue flags show what needs attention. Separate date reminders help with what’s coming next.

You get every core feature free for up to five events and reminders combined. Unlimited items sit in the US$0–$10 one-time band, with no subscription, account, ads, analytics or tracking. Your content stays on your device and in your own private iCloud.

Reading the costs

Each band includes its upper amount; the next starts above it. One-time bands use the published purchase amount. Yearly bands use a published annual option. Local prices and offers can differ. Monthly and weekly payments may cost more over a year. We leave a band Unclear when public information does not reliably tie an amount to its billing period.

Comparisons that start with your day

What do you need to remember?

When you last did something

For filters, haircuts, chores and other things without a rigid schedule, look at reset history, average or typical intervals, and how the app decides something is due. Time Since, Last Time, SinceWhen, Wheneri, Chronicling, DoneAgo, Since and SinceNow approach this in different ways.

The next important date

For birthdays, holidays and milestones, compare countdowns, annual repeats, reminders and widgets. Time Since includes a separate reminders list. DayCount, Countdown Star, Countdown and Event Countdown put dated events at the centre.

A simple one-time purchase

Time Since and Last Time sit in the lowest one-time band. Some general countdown apps do too. Several trackers offer a lifetime purchase alongside subscriptions; compare what each unlock actually includes.

A quieter privacy choice

Look for the difference between no developer data collection, usage/crash analytics, and advertising tracking. Time Since and SinceWhen declare no collection. Other apps’ collection and storage models differ, and a paid ad-removal option does not by itself establish that analytics stop.

01Time SinceEveryday events + dates · Costs, features & sources+

Free use and payment. Five saved events and reminders combined are free, with all core features included. Pro is a one-time purchase in the lowest cost band and removes that saved-item limit. Resetting an event does not use up a free action.

Features. Records each reset and its optional note, shows the average gap, and flags events Due or Overdue from that history. Separate reminders count down to dates and can send local alerts. Widgets link into the app; Siri can read elapsed days and the average interval.

Privacy. No account, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The App Store declaration says Data Not Collected. Content is saved on the device and synced through the user’s private iCloud. Learned event flags are visual; they do not trigger the date-reminder alerts.

How this was put together

Public sources. Practical differences.

How we chose the apps

Discovery covered Apple storefront searches and exact app-ID lookups across the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. We profiled 22 alternatives and selected twelve for this table alongside Time Since. The selection combines established names with closely related last-time trackers.

Ratings helped identify candidates; they are not downloads, active users, quality scores or evidence of Google search demand. This is a curated Apple-app comparison, not a global popularity ranking.

How we checked the claims

We read public store listings, provider features, pricing, help, privacy policies and release notes, and reviewed Time Since against its current implementation. Source conflicts and missing details remain explicit.

Reset history is different from changing a date. A typical rhythm is not automatically an arithmetic average. Learned due flags, manually chosen recurrence and notification alerts are separate behaviors. Apple privacy labels are developer declarations, not independent audits.

Comparison disclosure: Prepared by Time Since’s maker from public information checked on 6 November 2026. Competing apps were not installed or tested. Features, limits, offers and costs may change or vary by plan and country. Check the provider’s current purchase terms before choosing.
